# Privacy notice wording

Copy-paste text for the site's own privacy notice. It matches the suggested
policy content the plugin adds to Settings → Privacy, so the two do not drift
apart. Replace **[bracketed]** parts.

> ### Terms & Conditions acceptance
>
> When you accept our Terms & Conditions we record that acceptance so that
> both you and we can later establish which version of the terms was agreed,
> and when. This is an access-control and contractual-compliance record. It is
> not used for marketing, profiling, or automated decisions about you.
>
> **Legal basis.** We rely on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, because accepting the
> terms is necessary for the performance of our contract with you and for
> providing you access to the platform, and on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, our
> legitimate interests in platform security, accountability, and being able to
> demonstrate and defend what was agreed. We do **not** rely on consent for
> this processing, so you are not asked to consent to it and cannot withdraw
> consent; you may instead object on the grounds described below.
>
> **What we record.** Your user account identifier, and your username and
> email address as they stood at that moment; the date and time in UTC
> together with the local time and time zone shown to you; the identifier,
> number and effective date of the version you accepted; the action you took;
> the site the acceptance was made on; your IP address; and your browser
> identification (User-Agent).
>
> **What we do not collect.** We do not use device fingerprinting, and we do
> not collect MAC addresses or location data.
>
> **Who else receives it.** We email you a copy of the terms you accepted, as
> a receipt. That email is handled by **[your email provider]**, acting as our
> processor. **[If that provider processes data outside the EEA, describe the
> transfer safeguard here — for example EU Standard Contractual Clauses.]**
>
> **How long we keep it.** We keep acceptance records for as long as your
> account exists, and then for a further **[5]** years — the period in which a
> contractual claim could still be brought that the record would answer. After
> that we erase the IP address and browser identification, and keep the fact,
> version and time of acceptance as an accountability record. We keep every
> published version of the terms indefinitely, because an acceptance whose
> text cannot be reproduced has little evidential value.
>
> **Your rights.** You can ask for a copy of the acceptance records we hold
> about you. Because these records exist to establish and defend what was
> agreed, Article 17(3) GDPR limits your right to have them erased before the
> period above has elapsed; we will explain this if you ask us to delete them.
> You may object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and you may
> complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (**[www.dpa.gr]**).

## At the point of collection

The acceptance dialog already tells the user, in one line, that acceptance is
required to continue and is recorded with the time, their IP address and their
browser identification. Keep it if you customise the dialog — it is what makes
the collection transparent at the moment it happens (Art. 13).
